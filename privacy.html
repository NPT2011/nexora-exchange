Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information Nexora Exchange collects, why, and how it is protected.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: name, email address, country, mobile number and referral code.
  • Verification (KYC): document type and number, photos of the document, and a selfie.
  • Activity: deposits, withdrawals, wallet addresses, transaction IDs, orders, trades and P2P orders.
  • Messages: P2P chat messages and payment screenshots, and support chat messages.
  • Technical: login and security information such as IP address and device/browser details.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and process your transactions.
  • To verify identity, keep accounts secure, and prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To resolve P2P appeals and answer support requests.
  • To meet legal requirements.

3. Who we share it with

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Service providers that host and run the platform (website hosting and database) process data for us.
  • Deposits and withdrawals are recorded on public blockchains, which anyone can see.
  • In a P2P order, the other user sees your display name and the payment details you chose to share.
  • We may share information when the law requires it.

4. How we protect it

  • Identity documents and P2P screenshots are kept in private storage and can only be seen by you and authorised staff.
  • Admin access requires two-factor authentication.
  • No system is completely secure, so please protect your own password and 2FA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and afterwards for as long as needed for security, dispute and legal reasons.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see or correct your information, or ask us to close your account, by emailing nexoraxexchange@gmail.com. Some records may need to be kept as explained above.

7. Browser storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you logged in and remember settings such as favourite pairs. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
