6. Spot trading

  • Filled orders are final. Open orders can be cancelled until they are filled.

7. P2P trading

  • In P2P, users buy and sell USDT with each other for local currency. The seller's USDT is held in escrow by Nexora until the order is completed or cancelled.
  • The buyer must pay using the seller's listed payment method, upload a screenshot of the payment, and then press "I have paid".
  • The seller must check that the money has actually arrived before releasing. Release requires the account password and the Google Authenticator code, and cannot be undone.
  • If there is a dispute, either side can raise an appeal. Nexora support will review the chat and proof and decide whether to release the USDT to the buyer or return it to the seller.
  • Deals made outside the Nexora P2P order are not protected.
